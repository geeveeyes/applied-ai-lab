MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
between Orion Logistics Corp. ("Provider") and Contoso Health Partners ("Customer")

1. This Master Services Agreement commences on  the 21st
day of December, 2025.
2. This Agreement supersedes  the Order Form signed on
January 15, 2024.
3. The Agreement shall continue for a period of 18 months
from  the Effective Date (the "Initial Term").
4. Following  the Initial Term, this Agreement
automatically renews for successive one-year periods
unless either party gives notice of non-renewal.
5. Late payments accrue interest after a grace period of
10 days, and customers must give 90 days notice before
changing their billing contact.
6. Customer shall pay each invoice within forty-five (45)
days of receipt.
7. Termination without cause requires thirty (30) days
prior written notice.
8. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the
State of New York.
9. Nothing in this Agreement limits either party's
liability for breach of confidentiality.
10. Support credits are capped at $5,000 per quarter.
