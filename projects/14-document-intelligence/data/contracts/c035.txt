MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
between Helix Cloud Inc. ("Provider") and Contoso Health Partners ("Customer")

1. Effective as of  the 6th of April 2024,  the parties
enter into this Master Services Agreement.
2. This Agreement supersedes  the Order Form signed on May
27, 2023.
3. The initial term shall be thirty-six (36) months from
the Effective Date.
4. This Agreement is evergreen and renews automatically at
the end of each term.
5. Late payments accrue interest after a grace period of
10 days, and customers must give 90 days notice before
changing their billing contact.
6. Fees are invoiced monthly, payment terms Net 60.
7. Either party may terminate this Agreement for
convenience upon 60 days' written notice to  the other
party.
8. This Agreement shall be construed in accordance with
California law.
9. Aggregate liability under this Agreement is capped at
USD 1,200,000.
10. Support credits are capped at $5,000 per quarter.
