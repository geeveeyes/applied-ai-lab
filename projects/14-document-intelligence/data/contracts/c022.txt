MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
between Orion Logistics Corp. ("Provider") and Fabrikam Retail Group ("Customer")

1. Effective as of  the 18th of August 2024,  the parties
enter into this Master Services Agreement.
2. The initial term of this Agreement is 1 year from the
Effective Date.
3. This Agreement shall not renew automatically; any
renewal requires a written amendment signed by both
parties.
4. Fees are invoiced monthly, payment terms Net 30.
5. Termination without cause requires sixty (60) days
prior written notice.
6. This Agreement shall be construed in accordance with
California law.
7. Each party's aggregate liability is limited to  the fees
paid in  the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.
