MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
between Bramble Analytics LLC ("Provider") and Litware Education Corp. ("Customer")

1. Effective as of the 18th of May 2025, the parties enter
into this Master Services Agreement.
2. The Agreement shall continue for a period of 18 months
from the Effective Date (the "Initial Term").
3. This Agreement shall not renew automatically; any
renewal requires a written amendment signed by both
parties.
4. Fees are invoiced monthly, payment terms Net 30.
5. Either party may terminate this Agreement for
convenience upon 60 days' written notice to the other
party.
6. This Agreement shall be construed in accordance with
Delaware law.
7. Neither party's total liability shall exceed
$1,200,000.
