MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
between Marlow Data Systems Inc. ("Provider") and Fabrikam Retail Group ("Customer")

1. Effective Date: 2024-12-23. This Master Services
Agreement is entered into on the Effective Date.
2. This Agreement supersedes the Order Form signed on
January 26, 2023.
3. The initial term shall be twenty-four (24) months from
the Effective Date.
4. Renewal occurs only by mutual written agreement, and
the Agreement expires at the end of the term otherwise.
5. Late payments accrue interest after a grace period of
10 days, and customers must give 90 days notice before
changing their billing contact.
6. Customer shall pay each invoice within forty-five (45)
days of receipt.
7. Either party may terminate this Agreement for
convenience upon 30 days' written notice to the other
party.
8. This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and
Wales.
9. Each party's aggregate liability is limited to the fees
paid in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.
10. Support credits are capped at $5,000 per quarter.
