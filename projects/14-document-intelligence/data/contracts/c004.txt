MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
between Marlow Data Systems Inc. ("Provider") and Northwind Foods Inc. ("Customer")

1. This Master Services Agreement commences on the 9th day
of February, 2023.
2. This Agreement supersedes the Order Form signed on May
5, 2022.
3. The Agreement remains in force for 36 months after the
Effective Date.
4. Renewal occurs only by mutual written agreement, and
the Agreement expires at the end of the term otherwise.
5. Late payments accrue interest after a grace period of
10 days, and customers must give 90 days notice before
changing their billing contact.
6. All invoices are due 15 days after the invoice date.
7. Either party may end this Agreement on 30 days' notice,
for any reason.
8. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the
State of California.
9. Neither party's total liability shall exceed
$1,200,000.
10. Support credits are capped at $5,000 per quarter.
