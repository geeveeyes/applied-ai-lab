MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
between Bramble Analytics LLC ("Provider") and Contoso Health Partners ("Customer")

1. This Master Services Agreement is effective as of
November 15, 2025 (the "Effective Date").
2. The initial term shall be thirty-six (36) months from
the Effective Date.
3. Renewal occurs only by mutual written agreement, and
the Agreement expires at the end of the term otherwise.
4. All invoices are due 15 days after the invoice date.
5. Either party may terminate this Agreement for
convenience upon 60 days' written notice to the other
party.
6. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the
State of California.
7. Nothing in this Agreement limits either party's
liability for breach of confidentiality.
