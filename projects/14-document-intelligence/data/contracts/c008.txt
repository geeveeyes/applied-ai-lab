MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
between Orion Logistics Corp. ("Provider") and Northwind Foods Inc. ("Customer")

1. This Master Services Agreement is effective as of March
17, 2025 (the "Effective Date").
2. The initial term shall be twelve (12) months from the
Effective Date.
3. This Agreement is evergreen and renews automatically at

prior written notice.
6. This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and
Wales.

paid in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.
