MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
between Orion Logistics Corp. ("Provider") and Tailspin Travel Co. ("Customer")

1. This Master Services Agreement is dated 04/03/2025 and
takes effect on that date.
2. This Agreement supersedes  the Order Form signed on
February 1, 2024.
3. The Agreement remains in force for 18 months after the
Effective Date.
4. Renewal occurs only by mutual written agreement, and
the Agreement expires at  the end of  the term otherwise.
5. Late payments accrue interest after a grace period of
10 days, and customers must give 90 days notice before
changing their billing contact.
6. All invoices are due 60 days after  the invoice date.
7. Either party may end this Agreement on 60 days' notice,
for any reason.
8. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the
State of Texas.
9. Neither party's total liability shall exceed $250,000.
10. Support credits are capped at $5,000 per quarter.
