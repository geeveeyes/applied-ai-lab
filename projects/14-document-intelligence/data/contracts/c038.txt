between Bramble Analytics LLC ("Provider") and Adatum Energy LLC ("Customer")

28, 2023 (the "Effective Date").
2. The initial term shall be twenty-four (24) months from
the Effective Date.
3. This Agreement is evergreen and renews automatically at

convenience upon 90 days' written notice to  the other

New York law.

$1,200,000.
