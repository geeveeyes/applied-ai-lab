MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
between Quill Software Ltd. ("Provider") and Contoso Health Partners ("Customer")

1. This Master Services Agreement is effective as of
September 15, 2024 (the "Effective Date").
2. The initial term shall be eighteen (18) months from the
Effective Date.
3. Following the Initial Term, this Agreement
automatically renews for successive one-year periods
unless either party gives notice of non-renewal.
4. Fees are invoiced monthly, payment terms Net 60.
5. Either party may terminate this Agreement for
convenience upon 30 days' written notice to the other
party.
6. This Agreement shall be construed in accordance with
Texas law.
7. Aggregate liability under this Agreement is capped at
USD 250,000.
