MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
between Orion Logistics Corp. ("Provider") and Fabrikam Retail Group ("Customer")

1. Effective as of the 20th of August 2025, the parties
enter into this Master Services Agreement.
2. The Agreement remains in force for 18 months after the
Effective Date.
3. This Agreement shall not renew automatically; any
renewal requires a written amendment signed by both
parties.
4. All invoices are due 60 days after the invoice date.
5. Either party may end this Agreement on 60 days' notice,
for any reason.
6. This Agreement shall be construed in accordance with
Delaware law.
7. Aggregate liability under this Agreement is capped at
USD 250,000.
