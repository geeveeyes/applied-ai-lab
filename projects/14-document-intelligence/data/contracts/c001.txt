MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
between Orion Logistics Corp. ("Provider") and Contoso Health Partners ("Customer")

1. This Master Services Agreement is effective as of
November 2, 2024 (the "Effective Date").
2. The Agreement shall continue for a period of 12 months
from  the Effective Date (the "Initial Term").
3. This Agreement shall not renew automatically; any
renewal requires a written amendment signed by both
parties.
4. Customer's payment obligation arises 15 days following
the date of each invoice.
5. Either party may terminate this Agreement for
convenience upon 60 days' written notice to  the other
party.
6. This Agreement is governed by  the laws of England and
Wales.
7. Each party's aggregate liability is limited to  the fees
paid in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.
