between Bramble Analytics LLC ("Provider") and Fabrikam Retail Group ("Customer")

1. This Master Services Agreement is dated 03/10/2025 and
takes effect on that date.

3. The initial term of this Agreement is 3 years from the

automatically renews for successive one-year periods
unless either party gives notice of non-renewal.

10 days, and customers must give 90 days notice before
changing their billing contact.

prior written notice.

California law.
9. Aggregate liability under this Agreement is capped at
USD 250,000.
10. Support credits are capped at $5,000 per quarter.
