MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
between Marlow Data Systems Inc. ("Provider") and Contoso Health Partners ("Customer")

1. Effective as of the 13th of February 2024, the parties
enter into this Master Services Agreement.
2. The Agreement shall continue for a period of 24 months
from the Effective Date (the "Initial Term").
3. Renewal occurs only by mutual written agreement, and
the Agreement expires at the end of the term otherwise.
4. Fees are invoiced monthly, payment terms Net 30.
5. Either party may terminate this Agreement for
convenience upon 90 days' written notice to the other
party.
6. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the
State of Delaware.
7. Neither party's total liability shall exceed
$1,200,000.
