MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
between Marlow Data Systems Inc. ("Provider") and Contoso Health Partners ("Customer")

1. Effective Date: 2024-05-08. This Master Services
Agreement is entered into on the Effective Date.
2. The initial term of this Agreement is 1 year from the
Effective Date.
3. Following the Initial Term, this Agreement
automatically renews for successive one-year periods
unless either party gives notice of non-renewal.
4. Customer's payment obligation arises 45 days following
the date of each invoice.
5. Termination without cause requires sixty (60) days
prior written notice.
6. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the
State of California.
7. Neither party's total liability shall exceed $500,000.
