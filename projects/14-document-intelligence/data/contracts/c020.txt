between Quill Software Ltd. ("Provider") and Fabrikam Retail Group ("Customer")

1. Effective Date: 2024-06-11. This Master Services
Agreement is entered into on the Effective Date.
2. This Agreement supersedes the Order Form signed on July
19, 2023.
3. The initial term of this Agreement is 2 years from the
Effective Date.
4. Following the Initial Term, this Agreement
automatically renews for successive one-year periods
unless either party gives notice of non-renewal.

10 days, and customers must give 90 days notice before
changing their billing contact.

7. Either party may end this Agreement on 30 days' notice,
for any reason.
8. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the
State of California.
9. Neither party's total liability shall exceed $250,000.
10. Support credits are capped at $5,000 per quarter.
