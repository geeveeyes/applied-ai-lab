MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
between Helix Cloud Inc. ("Provider") and Litware Education Corp. ("Customer")

1. This Master Services Agreement is dated 11/16/2023 and
takes effect on that date.
2. The Agreement shall continue for a period of 24 months
from the Effective Date (the "Initial Term").
3. Renewal occurs only by mutual written agreement, and
the Agreement expires at the end of the term otherwise.

prior written notice.
6. This Agreement shall be construed in accordance with
Texas law.
7. Aggregate liability under this Agreement is capped at
USD 1,200,000.
