# Example Prompts

## Career Decision

```text
I am considering moving from a stable big tech role to an AI startup. I care about learning, compensation, family stability, and long-term career growth. What should I focus on this week?
```

## Weekend Planning

```text
I have 5 hours this weekend and want to make progress on AI portfolio projects without burning out. Help me choose what to do.
```

## Product Strategy

```text
I want to build an AI tool for engineering managers. Possible users are managers at startups, senior ICs, and directors. Help me pick the first wedge.
```

## Learning Plan

```text
I know basic prompting and Python. I want to learn agents, evals, RAG, and MCP. Create a practical one-month plan.
```

## Personal Tradeoff

```text
I have too many goals: fitness, AI learning, interview prep, family time, and writing online. Help me decide what matters for the next 7 days.
```
