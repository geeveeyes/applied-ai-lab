# Reviewing an investment case

Use these questions when preparing a full report. They are research prompts, not validated selection rules. The application supplies a small ticker-specific question list and the actual evidence packet; do not imply comprehensive sector coverage.

## Three checks that an owner can revisit

1. Describe a material operating risk using a dated observation and the exact supporting source URL.
2. State an observable condition that would change the view. Distinguish reported thresholds from your proposed review assumptions. Avoid arbitrary universal cutoffs.
3. Give a sourced future event date if supplied, or label a suggested review date as a planning assumption. Leave unavailable dates blank.

Use no more than three checks and four gaps. A source existing in the report does not prove that it supports a particular claim. Read the evidence. If evidence is absent, say exactly what is needed; never invent an observation. A review becoming due does not establish that a risk happened. New observations require new research, and frozen reports remain unchanged.

## Business-specific questions

- Amazon: separate AWS, retail and advertising. Distinguish capital spending, operating performance and the stock's price.
- Semiconductors: customer concentration, shared spending cycles, pricing power, capacity and indirect competitors.
- Lenders: comparable loan vintages, losses, underwriting, funding and regulatory capital. Corporate cash-flow valuation is unsuitable.
- Defense: contract execution, cost overruns, budget concentration, backlog conversion and cash collection.
- Other companies: cash sustainability, competitive position, dilution and operating results. Missing a specialized framework does not force a wait verdict.

## Evidence and valuation discipline

Reconcile adjusted measures to reported accounting instead of banning them. Treat stock compensation, existing awards and future dilution consistently; do not subtract the same cost twice. Keep equity cash flow separate from enterprise cash flow and use the corresponding discount rate. Calculations remain in code.

Historical case selection is not prospective validation. Do not call a hindsight-fit checklist calibrated or turn subjective scenario weights into success probabilities. To evaluate improvements, freeze recommendations with their evidence dates, compare subsequent total returns to an appropriate benchmark and include all cases, failures, dividends and costs.

## Personal portfolio context

A company can be attractive while adding to it worsens concentration. Consider direct and fund exposure, employer income and unvested awards, account type, liquidity needs and tax lots when supplied. State missing context explicitly. Do not infer taxes from incomplete cost basis or execute a trade.
