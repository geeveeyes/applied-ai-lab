# Procurement and Vendor Policy

Purchases under $1,000 can be made with a company card. Purchases between $1,000 and $10,000 need written approval from a director. Anything above $10,000 requires a signed contract reviewed by legal and approval from the VP of finance.

## Vendor security

New software vendors that handle company or customer data must complete a security questionnaire before purchase. Renewals are reviewed annually.
