# Expense Reimbursement Guide

Submit expenses through the finance portal within 30 days of purchase. Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to $75 per day.

## Travel

Economy airfare is the default. Flights longer than six hours may be booked in premium economy with director approval. Hotel stays are capped at $250 per night in major cities.

## Rejections

Expenses without receipts are rejected. Alcohol is never reimbursed. Appeals go to the finance operations team within 14 days.
