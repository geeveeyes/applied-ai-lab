# Benefits Overview

## Health coverage

Medical, dental and vision coverage starts on the first day of employment. The company pays 90 percent of the employee premium and 70 percent of dependent premiums.

## Retirement

The company matches 401(k) contributions dollar for dollar up to 4 percent of salary. Matching contributions vest immediately.

## Learning budget

Each employee has a $1,200 annual learning budget for courses, books and conferences. Unused budget does not roll over. Conference travel is billed to the expense guide rules.
