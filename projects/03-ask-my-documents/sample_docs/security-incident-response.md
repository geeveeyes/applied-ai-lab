# Security Incident Response

## Reporting

Report suspected incidents to the security team through the #security-incidents channel or by phone at the security hotline. Do not wait to confirm: report anything that looks like phishing, leaked credentials, or unexpected access.

## Severity levels

Severity 1 incidents involve customer data exposure or production compromise and require an incident commander within 30 minutes. Severity 2 incidents are contained internal issues and are triaged within 4 hours.

## After the incident

A blameless postmortem is due within 5 business days of resolution. Postmortems are shared with the whole engineering organization.
