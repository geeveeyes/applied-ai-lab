# Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. Core collaboration hours are 10am to 3pm local time.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop and a monitor. Home office stipends of $500 are available once every two years. Receipts must be submitted within 30 days.

## Security

All remote devices must use full-disk encryption and the company VPN when accessing internal systems. Lost devices must be reported to IT within 24 hours.
