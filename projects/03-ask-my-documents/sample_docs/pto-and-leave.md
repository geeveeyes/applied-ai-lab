# PTO and Leave Policy

## Vacation

Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid time off per year, earned monthly. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next calendar year. Requests of more than 5 consecutive days need manager approval at least 3 weeks ahead.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is separate from vacation and is unlimited with a doctor's note after 3 consecutive days. Tell your manager before the start of your shift when possible.

## Parental leave

Birth and adoptive parents receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Leave may be taken in one block or split into two blocks within the first 12 months.
