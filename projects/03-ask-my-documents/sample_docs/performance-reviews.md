# Performance Review Process

Reviews happen twice a year, in June and December. Each review combines a self-assessment, peer feedback from at least three colleagues, and a manager assessment.

## Ratings

Ratings use a four-point scale: exceeds, meets, partially meets, and does not meet expectations. Promotions are decided in the December cycle only.

## Calibration

Managers meet in calibration sessions to keep ratings consistent across teams. Employees can request a written summary of their calibration outcome within 2 weeks of receiving their rating.
